# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Things that help:

- Being kind and assuming good faith
- Accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the people who use this software
- Showing empathy toward other contributors

Things that do not:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome advances
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## A note specific to this project

Fillwright handles people's resumes and helps them apply for work — often at a
vulnerable moment. Contributions that would weaken the privacy guarantees, or
that treat users' data as a resource to be exploited, are out of scope
regardless of how they are framed. See CONTRIBUTING.md for the rules that are
not negotiable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening a private issue or contacting the
maintainers. Reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and
the privacy of the reporter will be respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that do not align with this
Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for
behaviour they deem inappropriate.
