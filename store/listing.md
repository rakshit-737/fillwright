## Description (long)

Fillwright fills job application forms from your resume, and keeps your data on your computer.

Import your resume once — PDF, Word or plain text. Fillwright reads it inside your browser and builds a profile you can check and edit. On an application page, click the Fillwright button (or press Alt+Shift+F). A panel lists every field it recognised, what it would fill in, and how sure it is. You choose what to fill. You press Submit.

WHAT IT DOES
• Reads your resume in the browser — nothing is uploaded.
• Recognises application fields by their labels, not just by their code, and works on Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Ashby, iCIMS, SmartRecruiters, LinkedIn Easy Apply and ordinary company career pages.
• Fills repeated education and experience blocks from the matching entries, and can add missing blocks with a clearly labelled "Add" button when you ask.
• Handles searchable and custom dropdowns, including lists that load as you type.
• Shows why it chose each value, lets you correct any field, and remembers your corrections for that website — on your computer.
• Undoes a fill in one click.
• Keeps several profiles (for example, one for software roles and one for security roles).

WHAT IT NEVER DOES
• It never submits an application. The final click is always yours.
• It never guesses work authorisation, visa status, demographics, salary or background answers. Those stay blank until you answer them yourself.
• It never overwrites something you typed, unless you turn that on.
• It never sends your resume or profile anywhere. Its pages are blocked from making network requests by their security policy.

PRIVACY AND SECURITY
• Everything is stored in your browser on this device. You can encrypt it with a passphrase, export it, or erase it.
• By default Fillwright can only read a page after you click its button. Assist and Smart modes, which offer help without a click, are optional and ask for Chrome's site-access permission first.
• Optional writing help uses Chrome's built-in, on-device model where available. It is off by default, shows you exactly which facts it may use, and never fills an answer until you approve it.
• No accounts, no analytics, no ads.

Fillwright is open source. The privacy policy and security model are published with the code.

## Single purpose (Privacy practices tab)

Fillwright fills job application forms with information from the user's own resume and profile, stored on their device, after the user reviews and approves each fill.
