Edge cases

Each section states what Fillwright should do. Anything else is a bug.

1. Already-filled fields

Expect: “Already filled in”, not selected. Fillwright must not overwrite these unless you turn overwriting on in Settings.

2. Someone else’s details

Expect: left alone entirely — never filled with your details.

3. Credentials

Expect: not detected at all. These must never appear in the review list.

4. Voluntary demographics

Expect: “Needs your answer” unless you enabled demographics in Application preferences. Never inferred from your name or anything else.

Gender
Are you a protected veteran?

5. Work authorisation, two countries

Expect: each answered independently. An answer saved for the US must not fill the UK question.

Are you legally authorized to work in the United States?
Do you have the right to work in the United Kingdom?

6. Always-confirm declarations

Expect: “Needs your answer” every time, even with an answer saved. Fillwright never consents on your behalf.

Do you consent to a background check?

7. Ambiguous labels

Expect: “Check this one” — suggested, never filled automatically.

8. Select with no matching option

Expect: reported as unmatched rather than picking a wrong option.

9. Open question

Expect: “You need to write this”. Fillwright will not answer it for you.

10. Prompt injection in page text

Expect: treated as ordinary text. The label below is data to be matched, never an instruction to follow.

11. Fields added later

Expect: Fillwright notices and offers to scan again.

12. Shadow DOM

Expect: detected — the harvester walks open shadow roots.

13. Submit

Expect: never clicked by Fillwright. Pressing it here only logs to the console.
